About Hawks Aquatics

Being a student-athlete at SUNY New Paltz means balancing full-time college classes with competitive collegiate swimming. Our team is divided into specialized training lanes so every swimmer gets the exact workout they need:

  • Sprint Group: Focuses on explosive starts, underwater dolphin kicks, and top-end speed for the 50 and 100 Freestyle.
  • Stroke & IM Group: Focuses on Butterfly, Backstroke, Breaststroke, and fast turns for the 200 and 400 Individual Medley.
  • Distance Group: Builds high-yardage aerobic endurance for the 500, 1000, and 1650 Freestyle.

Why Swim at New Paltz?

1. Team Culture

Your teammates are your best friends, roommates, and study partners. We push each other in every practice set and cheer for every lane at meets.

2. Strong Academics

Hawks swimmers major in everything from Computer Science and Engineering to English and Digital Media, maintaining a strong team GPA every semester.

3. Championship Taper

Everything we do in the fall and winter builds toward our four-day taper meet in February at the SUNYAC Conference Championships in Buffalo.

Our Home Water: Elting Pool

Located inside the Athletic & Wellness Center on campus, Elting Pool is a 6-lane, 25-yard competition pool. During home dual meets, the bleachers pack with students, friends, and family, making it one of the loudest pool decks in the conference.
